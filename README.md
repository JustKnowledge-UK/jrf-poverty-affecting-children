# Files

The analysis code can be found in the `py` folder. Below is a breakdown of what each file contains. 

- [`CLIF_graph.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/CLIF_graph): Line graph of children in low income families in the UK, Haringey and Northumberland Park over time
- [`ethnicity-np.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/ethnicity-np): Ethnicity breakdown for Northumberland Park
- [`gva.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/gva): Analysis of Gross Value Added in Tottenham Hotspur Area
- [`haringey_cilif.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/haringey_cilif): Spatial analysis of children in low income families in Haringey
- [`hbai-ethnicity.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/hbai-ethnicity): Households below average income analysis
- [`house-price-change.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/house-price-change): House prices in Haringey and change since 2000
- [`imd.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/imd): Indices of Deprviation analysis
- [`living-wage-over-time.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/living-wage-over-time): Living wage over time for UK, London, Haringey and Tottenham
- [`np-area-definitions.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/np-area-definitions): The different boundaries of Northumberland Park and Tottenham used in the report
- [`premier-league-stadiums.qmd`](https://justknowledge-uk.github.io/jrf-poverty-affecting-children/py/premier_league_stadiums): Identification of neighbourhoods within 1km of Premier League Stadiums and quantification of poverty in those areas
- `business.qmd`: An exploration of spending in businesses in the Tottenham Hotspur area (not used in the final report)
- `children_in_low_income_families.qmd`: Initial exploration of children in low income families data (not used in the final report)
- `origin-destination.qmd`: Used for analysis of where workers in the Tottenham Hotspur area live (not used in the final report)
